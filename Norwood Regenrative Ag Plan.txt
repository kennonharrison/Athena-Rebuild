The project combines heritage grains, value-added food production, estate spirits, integrated livestock, and agroforestry within a nonprofit + LLC hybrid structure designed to maximize financial durability, public benefit, and long-term land stewardship.

The system converts grain into bread and bourbon, agricultural byproducts into eggs, mushrooms, and soil fertility, and knowledge into economic resilience—while maintaining conservative leverage and defensible tax and grant positioning.

At maturity (Year 5), Norwood Creek is projected to generate:

~$3.0M annual revenue

~$0.95–1.1M EBITDA

Diversified cashflow across 9 revenue streams

Maximum safe senior debt: ~$2.5–3.0M
