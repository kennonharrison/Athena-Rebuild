# Knowledge File 2: Behavioral Questions Bank

## How to Use This File

Select questions based on the path:
- **Path A (Resume + Follow-ups):** Ask 3-4 questions ONLY from clusters where the resume left gaps. Focus on Clusters 2 and 5 (Interpersonal and Emotional/Professional Maturity), as these are hardest to infer from resumes.
- **Path B (Conversational):** Ask 10-12 questions covering ALL 5 clusters. Pick 2-3 from each cluster. Vary your selections -- don't always use the same questions.

**Style rules:**
- Ask ONE question at a time. Wait for the response before asking the next.
- Weave questions naturally into conversation. Don't say "Question 3 of 12."
- Use follow-up probes when answers are vague, short, or interesting.
- Adapt the scenario to be relevant to the user's stated situation (student, working, between jobs, etc.)

## CLUSTER 1: Communication & Expression
*Assesses: Verbal Communication, Delivery, Topic Development, Tone, Language Use*

**Q1.1:** "Your team just finished a project and the client isn't happy with the results. You need to explain what happened in a meeting. Walk me through how you'd handle that."
- Probes: "What would you say first?" / "How would you set the tone?" / "What if they get angry?"
- Signals: Structure of response (Delivery), tone calibration (Tone), clarity (Verbal Comm), how they develop the explanation (Topic Development)

**Q1.2:** "You have 60 seconds to explain what you do to someone who knows nothing about your field. Go."
- Probes: "Now do it for a 10-year-old." / "Now do it for a CEO."
- Signals: Clarity under pressure (Delivery), audience awareness (Tone, Formality), vocabulary range (Language Use)

**Q1.3:** "Tell me about the last time you had to write something important -- an email, a report, a proposal, anything. What made it important and how did you approach it?"
- Probes: "Did you revise it?" / "How did you decide on the tone?"
- Signals: Written communication care (Language Use, Formality), process (Organization), audience awareness (Tone)

**Q1.4:** "Have you ever had to deliver bad news to someone? How did you do it?"
- Probes: "How did they react?" / "Would you do it differently now?"
- Signals: Emotional calibration (Tone), structure (Delivery), empathy crossover (Empathy)

**Q1.5:** "Describe something you're passionate about -- anything -- to me like I've never heard of it."
- Probes: "What makes it interesting to you?" / "Why should I care?"
- Signals: Topic Development, enthusiasm/Delivery, Verbal Communication, Persuasion crossover

## CLUSTER 2: Interpersonal & Collaboration
*Assesses: Collaboration, Empathy, Perspective Taking, Trust, Assertiveness*

**Q2.1:** "You're working on a group project and one person isn't pulling their weight. Everyone notices but no one says anything. What do you do?"
- Probes: "Do you talk to them directly or go to the leader?" / "What if they get defensive?" / "What if they have something going on you don't know about?"
- Signals: Assertiveness (direct vs. avoidant), Empathy (considers their situation), Collaboration (team-focused vs. self-focused)

**Q2.2:** "Tell me about a time you worked with someone whose approach was completely different from yours. How did it go?"
- Probes: "What did you learn from them?" / "Did you change anything about how you work?"
- Signals: Perspective Taking, Adaptability crossover, Collaboration

**Q2.3:** "Has someone at work or school ever taken credit for your idea? What happened?"
- Probes: "How did it make you feel?" / "What did you do about it?" / "Would you handle it differently now?"
- Signals: Assertiveness, Emotional Regulation crossover, Trust

**Q2.4:** "Think about the best team you've ever been on. What made it work?"
- Probes: "What was your role in making it work?" / "What would have broken it?"
- Signals: Collaboration (do they emphasize team dynamics or personal contribution?), Perspective Taking

**Q2.5:** "Someone you work with is clearly having a terrible day. They're not performing well and it's affecting the team. What do you do?"
- Probes: "What if it keeps happening?" / "Where's the line between being supportive and enabling?"
- Signals: Empathy, Assertiveness, Perspective Taking, Trust

**Q2.6:** "Tell me about a time you had to trust someone you weren't sure about."
- Probes: "How did you decide to trust them?" / "Were you right?"
- Signals: Trust, Perspective Taking, Risk assessment

## CLUSTER 3: Drive & Self-Management
*Assesses: Initiative, Self-discipline, Responsibility, Resilience, Organization*

**Q3.1:** "Tell me about a time you had to figure something out with no instructions, no training, and no one to ask."
- Probes: "What was your first move?" / "How long did it take?" / "What would you do differently?"
- Signals: Initiative (acted vs. waited), Self-discipline (persisted), Resilience (handled frustration)

**Q3.2:** "What's something you've stuck with for a long time even when it was hard or boring?"
- Probes: "What kept you going?" / "Did you ever almost quit?"
- Signals: Self-discipline, Resilience, Initiative

**Q3.3:** "You have three deadlines on the same day. One is for your boss, one is for a teammate who's counting on you, and one is a personal commitment. What do you do?"
- Probes: "Which comes first?" / "Do you tell anyone you're overloaded?"
- Signals: Organization (prioritization), Responsibility (ownership), Assertiveness crossover (communicating limits)

**Q3.4:** "Tell me about your biggest professional or academic failure. What happened?"
- Probes: "What did you take away from it?" / "How long did it take to recover?"
- Signals: Responsibility (owns vs. externalizes), Resilience (recovery), Confidence crossover

**Q3.5:** "If I gave you a month with no obligations -- no work, no school, no responsibilities -- what would you do?"
- Probes: "Why that?" / "Would you plan it out or go with the flow?"
- Signals: Initiative (proactive vs. passive), Curiosity crossover, Organization (plans vs. drifts)

## CLUSTER 4: Adaptability & Growth
*Assesses: Adaptability, Curiosity, Creativity, Aesthetic Appreciation*

**Q4.1:** "Your job or program suddenly changes completely. New tools, new expectations, new people. You have two weeks to get up to speed. How do you approach it?"
- Probes: "What do you do on day one?" / "What if you're still struggling on day 10?"
- Signals: Adaptability (comfort with change), Initiative crossover, Self-discipline crossover

**Q4.2:** "What's the last thing you learned just because you were curious about it? Not for work or school -- just because."
- Probes: "How deep did you go?" / "Did it connect to anything else?"
- Signals: Curiosity (pure), Creativity crossover (connections)

**Q4.3:** "Describe a problem you solved in a way that surprised people -- or surprised yourself."
- Probes: "Where did the idea come from?" / "Did anyone push back?"
- Signals: Creativity, Initiative, Confidence crossover

**Q4.4:** "You're asked to improve something that everyone says is fine the way it is. What do you do?"
- Probes: "How do you make the case for change?" / "What if they say no?"
- Signals: Creativity, Assertiveness crossover, Persuasion crossover, Initiative

**Q4.5:** "Is there something -- a product, a place, an experience -- that you think is just beautifully designed or well-crafted? What makes it stand out to you?"
- Probes: "What specifically about it?" / "Do you notice that kind of thing often?"
- Signals: Aesthetic Appreciation, Curiosity crossover
- Note: Only ask this if the conversation flows naturally here. It's the least critical cluster element.

## CLUSTER 5: Emotional & Professional Maturity
*Assesses: Emotional Regulation, Confidence, Persuasion*

**Q5.1:** "Someone publicly disagrees with your idea in a meeting -- or in class, or in front of a group. What's your move?"
- Probes: "What's going through your head in that moment?" / "What if they're right?" / "What if they're wrong and being rude about it?"
- Signals: Emotional Regulation (composure), Confidence (stands ground vs. crumbles), Assertiveness crossover

**Q5.2:** "Have you ever had to change someone's mind about something important? Tell me about it."
- Probes: "What approach did you take?" / "What worked?" / "How long did it take?"
- Signals: Persuasion, Perspective Taking crossover, Confidence

**Q5.3:** "What are you genuinely great at? And what are you honestly not that good at?"
- Probes: "How do you know?" / "Does the thing you're not good at bother you?"
- Signals: Confidence (self-awareness without self-deprecation or arrogance), Emotional Regulation

**Q5.4:** "Tell me about a time you had to stay calm in a situation that was really stressful or unfair."
- Probes: "What was going through your mind?" / "Did anyone notice you were keeping it together?"
- Signals: Emotional Regulation, Resilience crossover

**Q5.5:** "You believe strongly that something should be done differently, but the person in charge disagrees. What do you do?"
- Probes: "How far do you push it?" / "At what point do you let it go?"
- Signals: Persuasion, Assertiveness crossover, Emotional Regulation, Confidence

---

## FOLLOW-UP PROBES (Universal)

Use these anytime an answer is thin:
- "Tell me more about that."
- "What was the hardest part?"
- "What would you do differently now?"
- "How did that feel?"
- "What did you learn from that?"
- "What happened next?"
- "Why did you choose that approach over [alternative]?"
