# ETS Applied Intelligence
## Studio Overview

---

### What It Is
ETS Applied Intelligence leverages the trust ETS has built over 75+ years of service to the world's talent development ecosystems to drive value for people, governments, and employers at global scale.

It is an internal venture studio that builds, operates, and scales commercial products on top of ETS's measurement science and trust infrastructure. ETS remains the standard-setter. The studio builds the operating layer.

---

### Why Now
- AI is collapsing the distance between assessment, learning, and hiring
- ETS has the measurement IP but not the operating model to capture the commercial layer
- Competitors (Coursera, LinkedIn, Google) are building vertically -- if ETS doesn't operationalize, it becomes a commodity input
- Three ventures are already in motion and need a governance home

### The Thesis

ETS is the most trusted name in human measurement. But trust infrastructure alone doesn't capture value -- operating systems do.

**Layer 1 (ETS):** Sets standards. Validates skills. Issues credentials. Maintains independence.

**Layer 2 (Studio):** Builds the products and platforms that put Layer 1 to work -- certification platforms, degree programs, talent exchanges.

The certifier and the operator must be structurally separate. That separation is a feature: it preserves ETS's neutrality while allowing the studio to compete commercially.

Every studio venture uses ETS IP as its foundation:
- Certify.ai uses ETS psychometrics and Praxis distribution
- Khan University uses Futurenav for skills assessment
- ASP/ProFound uses Futurenav Skillprints as its matching layer

The studio doesn't compete with ETS. It makes ETS indispensable.
